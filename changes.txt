TERRA FRONT - CHANGES

Version 0.9.3

- Pause: F1 or STOP pauses the game; press either again to go on.
- The enemy formation's loop is flatter and sits lower on the screen.

Version 0.9.2

- High score list: the ten best scores, with initials entered at the
  end of the game (keyboard, or joystick alone). The title shows the
  best score, and the list between the title and the game playing
  itself. The disk version keeps the list in TERRAFNT.HSC.

Version 0.9.1

- Easter egg adjustments.

Version 0.9.0

- First version to play: the front, reserve ships and call-ups, ten
  enemies in waves, bosses after waves 5 and 10 of each level, the
  score, bonus ships, the title screen and attract mode.
- Also as a disk version: TERRAFNT.DSK starts by itself; TERRAFNT.BIN
  can be BLOADed from Disk BASIC.
