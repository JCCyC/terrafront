TERRA FRONT - CHANGES

Version 0.9.8

- The boss after wave 10 is easier: its line has three enemy pairs
  instead of four (it sends down one more to make up for it).

Version 0.9.7

- Playtesting adjustments.

Version 0.9.6

- When the game plays by itself, every other time it starts with a
  boss, the two bosses in turn.

Version 0.9.5

- A new boss after wave 10 of each level: it stays at the top of the
  screen behind a line of enemy pairs that swerves left and right, and
  sends down new pairs as they fall. It takes hits and changes colour
  like the boss after wave 5, and gives the same bonus. When it is
  destroyed, every enemy and bullet left vanishes.
- The cartridge image is now 32 KB (it was 16 KB).

Version 0.9.4

- Free enemies bouncing off the bottom of the screen no longer fly
  away almost level: they turn up at least 30 degrees.

Version 0.9.3

- Pause: F1 or STOP pauses the game; press either again to go on.
- The enemy formation's loop is flatter and sits higher on the screen.

Version 0.9.2

- High score list: the ten best scores, with initials entered at the
  end of the game (keyboard, or joystick alone). The title shows the
  best score, and the list between the title and the game playing
  itself. The disk version keeps the list in TERRAFNT.HSC.

Version 0.9.1

- Easter egg adjustments.

Version 0.9.0

- First version to play: the front, reserve ships and call-ups, ten
  enemies in waves, bosses after waves 5 and 10 of each level, the
  score, bonus ships, the title screen and attract mode.
- Also as a disk version: TERRAFNT.DSK starts by itself; TERRAFNT.BIN
  can be BLOADed from Disk BASIC.
